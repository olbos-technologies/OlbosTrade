## Unreleased — 0DTE recorder: real quotes and 5/10-minute marks for SPY and QQQ debit spreads (off by default, read-only)

- Free historical data has no 0DTE option quotes, and a model-priced backtest is weakest exactly here, so this collects our own. When `ZERO_DTE_RECORDER_ENABLED=true`, a 60-second scheduler tick takes an observation at 10:00, 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 13:00 and 14:00 ET: for SPY and QQQ it asks the 0DTE proposal logic what it would have proposed in each direction and stores the real leg quotes (or the refusal and its reasons, since how often the market is too wide or stale to trade is itself a finding). Five and ten minutes later it re-quotes the same two legs and stores the closing prices, so every observation carries its real round-trip cost. A mark whose chain cannot be fetched is retried until its grace ends and then left missing, never back-filled; an unreadable quote is recorded as unreadable.
- Read-only: it asks the broker for market data only, at the scans' priority (so it cannot delay an order or a risk check), and imports nothing that can reach an order path, which a test pins. Each request is a small windowed chain fetch (a few strikes near the money on one side, or both sides at observation), not the whole chain.
- New table `zero_dte_observations` (migration 0045). New script `backend/scripts/zero_dte_recorder_report.py` prints refusal rates, the net result per share after real costs at 5 and 10 minutes, the cost of crossing the spread, and a both-directions baseline, and says INSUFFICIENT DATA below 30 marks instead of reading anything into noise.
- Needs a backend restart and `alembic upgrade head` (the standard deploy does both); the new environment variable is optional and defaults to off, so nothing changes until it is set. Requires the 0DTE proposal core change (previous fragment) to be merged first.
