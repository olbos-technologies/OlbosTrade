## Unreleased — One Chart page: the Pro Workspace replaces the old Chart page

- **Markets > Chart** is now the former Pro Workspace: a chart in the middle with drawing tools, an Indicators picker and the volume profile, a side column and a bottom dock (watchlist, positions, unusual options, news, scanners, backtest), resizable and remembered in the browser. The separate **Pro Workspace** menu entry is gone, and the old address `/terminal/markets/workspace` redirects to Chart and keeps `?symbol=`. Links to `/terminal/markets/chart?symbol=XYZ` open the same page.
- **Removed with the old Chart page** (not rebuilt here): the instrument bar with its signal badge and detail drawer, the signal Entry/Stop/Target levels and catalyst chips on the chart, and its own positions/watchlist side panels (the workspace has watchlist and positions panels you can place in any slot). The compact chart in the Equity and Options desks is untouched. The indicator and volume-profile choices were already shared, so they carry over.
- **Phone, chart first:** the chart now takes about 80% of the screen (the canvas was about 210px tall, now about 450px), the OHLC line is dropped from the bar, its buttons are 44px, and the range buttons scroll in one row. Panels other than the chart are still one-at-a-time from the strip.
- Frontend only: needs a frontend rebuild; no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables. Not tested on a real phone, only in Chromium at 320 and 390px with real touch events.
