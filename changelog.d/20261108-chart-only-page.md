## Unreleased — the desktop chart page is just the chart

- The research dock under the chart (Setup readiness, Watchlist, Signals, Positions, Plan & context, Research, Safety) and the Focus chart toggle are gone on desktop. The page is the instrument bar and a tall chart that fills the window.
- A Symbol box in the bar loads any symbol and pins it to the picker; an × next to the current symbol removes it from the picker and moves to the next one. This replaces the Watchlist tab.
- Setup Readiness is no longer shown on desktop (it only lived on this page). It is still on the phone layout. The positions, safety and plan values are available on the Trade Desk and Command Center pages.
- Signal Detail is a flat outline in every chart layout.
- Phones and the compact Equity and Options desk charts keep their existing layout, including Focus chart.

Frontend only: no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
