## Unreleased — Boxed tabs, toggles and desk header rows, on every page

- Tab rows, in-page toggles and the Trade Desk header rows now share the ENV and MODE chips' box style: 1px border, 3px radius, the same fill, mono capitals. This covers the tab row on every section that uses the shared tab bar (Signals, Risk, System, Account, Research, Scan, Backtest, News and Income strategies), the Trade Desk's Session strip, tab row and "Additional desk tools" toggle, and the in-page toggles (Options / Equities, the order and execution filters, the Options Flow view switch, All / Buy / Sell on Signals). The Session boxes take their border colour from their state, like the ENV chips. Navigation tabs show the active one in the brand colour; toggles and filters show theirs in amber.
- Rows share one flat background and one grey bottom rule, and boxes start at the same left edge as the ENV chip (before: a raised outlined Session box with a gradient, gold-tinted rules, sans-serif title-case tabs on the workspace pages, underlined tabs on Signals).
- On the Trade Desk at widths under 1600px the Session "Kill switch" box is hidden, so the strip stays on one line with no truncated values; the ENV row's KILL chip and the kill-switch control still show that state. At narrow desktop widths the kill-switch control may sit on its own row. On phones the tab and toggle boxes are at least 40px tall.
- Frontend-only: needs a frontend rebuild, no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
- The Orders and Execution header rows (and the Equity desk, Options desk and Copilot tool rails that share the style) use the same flat row and boxed controls. On Orders the filter chips (All, Pending, Submitted, …) were centered and are now left-aligned with the rows above; the Refresh button is a box; the table's text lines up with the boxes.
