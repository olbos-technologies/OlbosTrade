## Unreleased — Desk header rows are boxed like the ENV row

- On the Trade Desk (desktop), the Session strip (Session, Day P&L, Broker, Regime, Risk budget, Drawdown, Kill switch), the tab row (Overview … Settings) and the "Additional desk tools & execution settings" toggle are now bordered boxes like the ENV and MODE chips: 1px border, 3px radius, the same fill, a small dim label beside a bold value, one left edge. The Session boxes take their border colour from their state (green, amber, red), as the ENV chips do; the active tab keeps the brand colour. The rows share one flat background and one grey bottom rule (before: a raised outlined box with a gradient, a gold-tinted rule, and sans-serif text at a different inset). At narrower desktop widths the Session boxes wrap to a second line. Phone layout is unchanged. Frontend-only: needs a frontend rebuild, no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
