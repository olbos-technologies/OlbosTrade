## Unreleased — Multi-timeframe view: one symbol on two to four charts at different intervals

- New page **Markets > Multi-timeframe** (`/terminal/markets/multi`, in the sidebar and the phone menu). One symbol on two to four charts, each at its own bar interval (default Daily, 1h, 15m), each with a range that suits its interval (Daily 1Y, 1h 1M, 15m 5D and so on). Each chart has its own interval selector; a symbol box, the shared watchlist's first eight symbols and `?symbol=` pick the symbol for all of them. Three charts sit in a row on a wide screen, four make a 2×2 grid, a laptop-size window uses two columns, and on a phone each chart takes a row of its own.
- Under the controls, one sentence says in words where price sits against its 50-bar average across the timeframes ("above ... on all 3 timeframes", or "Mixed: above on 2 of 3, below on 1"), and each chart shows `MA20` and `MA50` with an arrow and a word. This is description of what has already happened, not a signal, and the page says so; it avoids the green and red that mean profit and loss. A chart without enough bars shows a dash and is left out of the sentence, which says how many were compared.
- Each chart stands on its own: one whose data fails says so for itself and the others still draw; one that returns nothing says so; nothing is drawn from made-up bars. Changing one chart's interval refetches only that chart; a slower, older answer never overwrites the newest pick. The charts are not synchronised in time or zoom, and the page says so. One **Indicators** button applies to every chart; the small tiles start with MA20, MA50 and volume and keep their own saved choice, separate from the Chart page. The layout is saved in the browser.
- Frontend only: needs a frontend rebuild; no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables. Opens a new Markets menu entry.
