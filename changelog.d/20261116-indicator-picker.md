## Unreleased — Indicator picker on the Chart page and the Pro Workspace

- A new **Indicators** button (in the Chart page's chart toolbar and the Pro Workspace's chart bar) opens a panel of checkboxes for what the chart draws. Overlays on the price chart: MA5, MA10, MA20, MA50, MA200, EMA9, EMA21, EMA50, VWAP and Bollinger bands. Panels below it: Volume, MACD, RSI (with guides at 30 and 70) and ATR. Up to 10 at once; none ticked is valid (candles only); **Reset to default** restores what the chart drew before. The choice is remembered in the browser and is shared by the Chart page and the Workspace.
- The default is exactly what the chart always drew (MA5, MA10, MA20, VWAP, volume, MACD), and a chart that is not given a selection, such as the compact Equity and Options desk charts, is unchanged. A different selection rebuilds the chart and keeps the zoom position.
- Accuracy is tested, not assumed: RSI is checked against all 19 published values of the classic Wilder worked example, and ATR, Bollinger bands and the averages against small cases worked by hand. A line that needs more bars than are loaded (MA200 on a short range) reads as a dash, never a made-up number. VWAP is cumulative over the bars loaded and its description says so. The panel says plainly that indicators describe past prices and are not signals, and their colours avoid the green and red that mean profit and loss.
- The panel opens in a portal so the chart toolbar (which scrolls sideways on a phone) cannot clip it, as a sheet above the bottom navigation on a phone with 44px rows. It is a non-modal dialog: Escape or a click outside closes it, Tab stays inside, focus returns to the button.
- Frontend only: needs a frontend rebuild; no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
