## Unreleased — Volume profile on the Chart page

- The volume profile (a histogram of where volume traded, for the bars in view, with the point of control and the value area) was only on Markets > Pro Workspace. It is now on Markets > Chart too, on by default: a **Volume profile** button at the right of the chart toolbar turns it off and on, and the readout (for example `Volume profile · 80 bars · POC 341.07 · value area 338.21–343.94`) appears in the chart legend. The choice is remembered in the browser and is the same saved choice as the Workspace, so turning it off in one turns it off in the other.
- On a phone the button is at the end of the toolbar, which scrolls sideways like the interval buttons, and is a full 44px touch target. The readout is kept to one line (truncated with an ellipsis; the full text is in the tooltip) because it sits above the chart and every extra line would push the chart down, and on the narrowest phones (340px and under) the line is dropped while the histogram and the toggle stay, so the chart still starts early enough on the first screen. The profile is approximate (built from bars, with each bar's volume spread across its high-low range) and is not a signal, as on the Workspace. The compact desk charts (Equity and Options) are unchanged. Drawing tools stay Workspace-only, on desktop.
- Frontend only: needs a frontend rebuild; no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
