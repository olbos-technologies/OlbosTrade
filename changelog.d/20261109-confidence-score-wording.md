## Unreleased — Signal confidence is shown as a score out of 100, not a percent

- A signal's "confidence" was shown as e.g. "72%", which reads as a 72% chance the trade wins. It is a model score used to rank and gate signals, so it is now shown as "72/100" (or "score 72" in compact spots), labelled "Confidence score", and the signal detail explains that it is not a win probability. Covers the signal cards, scan panels, detail drawer, copilot queue, execution monitor, signal tables, and the trading-style minimums ("need score 70"). Real probabilities (POP, VaR level, regime confidence) keep their percent sign. Frontend-only: needs a frontend rebuild, no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables. The underlying numbers and the thresholds that gate entries are unchanged.
