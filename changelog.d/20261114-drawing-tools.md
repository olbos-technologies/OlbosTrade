## Unreleased — Drawing tools on the Pro Workspace chart

- The Pro Workspace chart has a drawing rail: **trend line, horizontal line, vertical line, rectangle and Fibonacci retracement**, plus select and edit, a magnet that snaps to a bar's open, high, low or close, six colours and three line widths, and undo, redo, delete and clear all. Click twice (or press, drag and release) to draw; click a drawing to move it, drag its handles to change it. One drag is one undo step.
- Drawings are anchored to time and price, so they stay on the same candle and price through zoom, scroll, resize and a change of bar interval. They are saved per symbol in this browser only (not in your account, not on other devices), and stored data is validated on load.
- They are notes: no orders, no stops, nothing that feeds risk or execution. Mouse and pen only; the rail is not offered on the stacked phone layout.
- Opt-in on the chart component, so the Chart page and every other chart are unchanged. Not included: text notes, rays, channels, Fibonacci extensions, a measure tool, and drawings synced across devices. See `docs/PRO_WORKSPACE.md`.
