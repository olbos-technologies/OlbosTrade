## Unreleased — 0DTE proposals and the recorder now refuse quotes that are not live

- The app asks IBKR for market-data type 4 (delayed-frozen, free), so an account without a live market-data subscription gets option and stock quotes that are 15 minutes old and look exactly like live ones. For a 0DTE spread, and for a record of what it was worth 5 and 10 minutes later, that is the wrong data: nothing priced off it could have been traded. `OptionsChain` now carries `market_data_type` (IBKR sets it from the tickers' own `marketDataType`: 1 only if every quoted contract is live, otherwise the most delayed type seen; unset when the broker does not say), and the 0DTE proposal logic refuses any chain that is not known to be live, with the reason naming the data type. The recorder stores the data type on every observation and flags a delayed mark as unreadable instead of recording a price. Unknown freshness is treated as not live, so a broker that does not report it cannot slip through.
- Nothing else changes: equity and options scans, signals, orders and risk checks are untouched. If your IBKR account only has delayed data, the recorder will now say so in its refusals ("quotes are not live (market-data type 4 ...)") instead of quietly collecting 15-minute-old prices, and the fix is a live US options (OPRA) and stock data subscription on the IBKR account.
- Needs a backend restart; no migration, no new environment variables.
