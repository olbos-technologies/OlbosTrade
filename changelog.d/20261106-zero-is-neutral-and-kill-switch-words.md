## Unreleased — A flat day is no longer green, and the kill switch has one vocabulary (plus a hardcoded "OFF / OK" fixed)

- **Zero is neutral.** A day with no P&L printed "+$0.00" in green on the Command Center, and the same `x >= 0 ? green : red` rule was repeated across the app. A new shared helper (`utils/pnlTone.ts`) makes a figure that rounds to $0.00 (or 0.00%) neutral and unsigned, positive green and negative red, and an unreadable value faint instead of green (call sites used `(pnl ?? 0) >= 0`, so a failed read became a green zero). Applied to the P&L figures on the Command Center, phone home, Dashboard, Trade Desk, Risk Monitor, Chart workstation, Journal, Executive Summary, discovery rails, replay and portfolio insights. Strategy statistics (expectancy, EV) and percent-change displays elsewhere keep their old rule.
- **One kill-switch vocabulary: ENGAGED or CLEAR.** The same state read "NOT ARMED" in the status strip (engaged: "HALTED"), "CLEAR" on the Command Center card, "Clear" in the Trade Desk header and "OFF" in the Risk rules list. "NOT ARMED" could be read as "the protection is not ready". The strip now says ENGAGED / CLEAR; a failed read keeps its UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED wording and never falls back to CLEAR.
- **Fixed: the Risk rules list's Kill Switch row was hardwired "OFF / OK".** It read `kill_switch_engaged`, which `/api/guardrails/status` never returns, so it said OK even while the switch was engaged. The row now reads the real kill-switch state (CLEAR / OK, ENGAGED / Breach, or UNVERIFIED when the read fails).
- The execution-mode readout still says HALTED while the switch is engaged (that is trading's state, not the switch's).
- Display only: no change to the kill switch, guardrails, orders or risk limits. Frontend only, no migration.
