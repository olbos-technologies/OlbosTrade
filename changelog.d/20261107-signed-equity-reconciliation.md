## Unreleased — Reconciliation now compares equity positions by side, not just size

- **A flipped position is no longer invisible.** The position check summed `abs(quantity)` on both sides, so "long 10" and "short 10" were the same number. An exit that fires on top of a manual close, a stop that triggers after the position was already flat, or an entry sent the wrong way all leave a position of the *same size on the opposite side*, and every size-only check called that clean. The check now compares signed quantities for equities: the broker's long/short against the trade rows' `equity_long` / `equity_short`.
- **Reported, never repaired.** A direction mismatch is logged as `DIRECTION_MISMATCH` (CRITICAL), counted as `reconciliation.direction_mismatch`, emitted as a `reconciliation_direction_mismatch` event, shown in the reconciliation status routes as `direction_mismatch_tickers`, and makes the check unclean. It is deliberately kept out of the list the quantity auto-correct reads — resizing a flipped row to the broker's number would make the flip look settled — and out of untracked-position adoption. The operator-triggered full reconcile halts (`ReconciliationError`) on it, ahead of ordinary size drift.
- **Same-side size drift is unchanged.** Long 10 against a row of 7 is still a quantity mismatch and is still auto-corrected exactly as before.
- **What it does not judge.** Option spreads and any underlying with both equity and option exposure stay on the previous size check: a spread is several legs whose signs cancel or add by structure and no verified leg ledger exists for legacy rows, so a signed comparison there would be invented evidence. A row whose direction is not recognised is reported as `DIRECTION UNVERIFIED` (which makes the check unclean) and compared by size only — it is never assumed long.
- **Deployment:** backend restart only. No migration, no new environment variables, no frontend change. Deploy outside 09:30–16:00 ET. Nothing here blocks or halts trading by itself; it makes the existing periodic check able to see a wrong-side position.
