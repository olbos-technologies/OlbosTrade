## Unreleased — Journal: monthly P&L calendar

- The Journal's Monthly Review tab now opens with a month calendar of realized P&L by the day each trade closed, with previous/next month arrows, a signed amount on every day that had closed trades (colour is never the only cue), and a month total. Days with no closed trades show only the date; trades with an unknown P&L are left out, not counted as zero. The calendar also appears for a month where trades closed but none opened, which the old review called "no trades". A failed load now says "Monthly review unavailable" instead of "No trades this month". The existing tiles keep their entry-month totals, so the calendar total (by close date) can differ from "Total P&L". No ROI figure is shown: the journal has no account-capital basis to compute it from. Needs a backend restart (new `daily_pnl` field on `/api/journal/review/monthly/{month}`) and a frontend rebuild; no migration, no new environment variables.
