## Unreleased — Attribution report: payoff, results by confidence bucket, by trading style, by asset class

- **`scripts/trade_attribution_report.py` gains four sections** (read-only; same run command as before, no migration, no new dependencies):
  - **9 PAYOFF** — average win against average loss, the payoff ratio, and the win rate that ratio *requires* to break even, beside the actual win rate. A 90% win rate can still sit below break-even if the losses are large enough; neither number alone says which book this is. Shown for all closed trades and, when inherited positions exist, for the desk's own trades separately.
  - **10 BY CONFIDENCE BUCKET** — results by `signal_score` (`< 0.50`, `0.50-0.59`, `0.60-0.69`, `>= 0.70`) for desk-originated trades, so a routing threshold can be read off a table instead of inferred from one AUC number. A recorded score of 0 (the old "none supplied" fallback) is filed as unscored, not as a low score; a score exactly on a boundary belongs to the higher bucket, so a trade at the 0.70 bar reads as meeting it.
  - **11 BY TRADING STYLE** (`trading_mode_at_entry`) and **12 BY ASSET CLASS** (equity vs options, which are sized and exited by different rules).
- **Same discipline as the rest of the report:** every mean carries a date-clustered bootstrap interval; cells under 30 closed trades are marked NOT INTERPRETABLE; buckets are only compared when at least two reach 30, and only called separated when their intervals do not overlap. With a young journal the report says plainly that the buckets cannot be compared, and that this is *not* evidence that a lower routing bar is safe or unsafe.
- **Deployment:** the script ships in the backend image, so it needs the normal backend rebuild/restart to be available on the server. It places no orders, calls no broker, writes nothing and prints no credentials. Deploy outside 09:30–16:00 ET.
