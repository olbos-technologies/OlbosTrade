## Unreleased — Kill-switch halts queued entries, IBKR cancels are confirmed, unknown claims clear on broker evidence

- **A halt now stops entries that were already in flight.** The kill switch carries an entry epoch; an entry captures a permit when it is dispatched and the permit is checked again immediately before the broker call, after every await. Engaging the switch invalidates permits held by requests, queued IBKR jobs and retry loops, and resetting the switch does not revive them. Manual closes and emergency flattening carry no entry permit and stay available. An order submission nobody classified is treated as an entry.
- **IBKR no longer cancels, sleeps and retries blind.** A limit order is replaced only after the broker acknowledges a zero-fill cancellation (`Cancelled` / `ApiCancelled`; `Inactive` is not enough). Bracket children must also be confirmed terminal and unfilled before the parent is replaced, a parent that fills while the cancel is pending keeps its protective children, and a terminal partial fill is never replaced. If the outcome is still unknown after a shared 10 s bound the order is reported `pending`, not `cancelled`, and the entry's claim is marked `unknown`.
- **Unknown claims no longer wait for a person.** IBKR cannot be asked about a client order id, so these claims could only be released by hand. Every position-reconciliation cycle now releases an unresolved claim on broker evidence only: the broker holds the position and an open trade row records it, or the broker holds nothing, no open/pending row exists, the claim is past the 120 s settle window, and a fresh network read (`confirm_symbol_flat`: positions and working orders, not the local cache) shows neither. Time alone, a failed read, a position with no recording row and a pending row all leave the claim in place. Each automatic release writes a `claim_resolved` event naming the evidence; manual release (`claim_override`) is unchanged and remains the last resort.
- **Not included:** the reconciliation entry gate (a fresh reconcile before every entry send) is deliberately left out of this change and will arrive separately with `observe` and `enforce` modes. Equity partial fills are still reported `filled` with the counts only in the message and recorded at the planned quantity.
- **Deployment:** backend restart only. No migration, no new environment variables, no frontend change. Deploy outside 09:30–16:00 ET.
