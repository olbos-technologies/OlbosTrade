## Unreleased — The Copilot approval queue no longer grows without bound ("5,480 approvals awaiting review")

- Cause: `_queue_pending_approval` appended a row for every qualifying scan while the desk was in Copilot (the same ~60 tickers every 15–30 minutes, for days) and nothing ever retired one. The list the operator was meant to review was thousands of stale copies of old signals, each with a scan-time price and spread that no longer existed, and `/api/trade-desk/pending` returned all of them on every poll.
- Now a newer scan of the same decision (ticker + asset class + action + strategy) **supersedes** the older pending one, and a pending approval older than `APPROVAL_TTL_MINUTES` (default 180) **expires**. Both keep the row as the audit trail, with status `superseded` / `expired` and a note saying why (and by which signal); neither is ever executed. BUY and SELL on one ticker, or two strategies on one ticker, are different decisions and are not merged.
- The existing backlog clears itself: the first `/pending` poll after deploy expires everything older than the TTL, and a stale approval is never offered even if that write fails. Tidying is best-effort and never prevents a new signal from being queued. A superseded or expired approval can no longer be approved (the route answers 404, as for any resolved one); `reject-all` still works.
- The options funnel's "Queued for approval" stage now also shows `superseded` and `expired` counts.
- No change to approving or executing a signal, to the gates an approval passes before it executes, or to Autopilot. Backend restart, no migration; new optional variable `APPROVAL_TTL_MINUTES`.
