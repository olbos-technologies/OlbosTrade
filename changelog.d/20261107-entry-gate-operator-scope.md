## Unreleased — Fix: the entry gate now reconciles the operator's whole book, not just unattributed rows

- **Bug in the gate shipped in the previous change (observe mode only; nothing was ever blocked).** The gate compared IBKR's positions against *unattributed* trade rows only. A desk whose open positions are attributed to the designated operator organization (`OPERATOR_ORGANIZATION_ID`) therefore saw every one of them as untracked: a live drill reported all three open positions that way while the periodic reconciliation, correctly, said they were in sync. In `observe` that would have written a `would_veto` record on every entry attempt (noise); in `enforce` it would have vetoed every entry.
- **Fix:** the gate now uses exactly the scope the periodic reconciliation sweep uses: with a designated operator, that organization's rows *plus* the unattributed rows are one scope (they share one broker); with none designated, the unattributed rows. Another organization's rows still never make the operator's position tracked. The pending-row lookup uses the same scope, and every record and the drill route now name the scope they were made in (`single-operator` or the organization id).
- **Why the tests missed it:** they seeded unattributed rows and a different organization's rows, never the production shape. New real-PostgreSQL tests use rows attributed to the designated operator, unattributed rows under a designation, another organization's rows, and the undesignated case; 14 of the new tests fail on the previous version.
- **Deployment:** backend restart only. No migration, no new environment variables. Deploy outside 09:30–16:00 ET. After deploying, `GET /api/admin/entry-gate` should report `"clean": true` for the three open positions.
