## Unreleased — Reconciliation entry gate for IBKR entries: off, observe (default), enforce

- **New gate, observing only by default.** Before each IBKR entry order is sent (options and equity, including replacement attempts after a confirmed cancel), the desk now checks whether its picture of the account agrees with the broker's. `RECONCILIATION_ENTRY_GATE_MODE` is `off`, `observe` (the default) or `enforce`. In `observe` nothing is blocked: what *would* have been vetoed, and every check that could not be made, is written to `execution_events` (kind `entry_gate`) with its scope, the entry's ticker, categorised reasons (`untracked_at_broker`, `phantom_in_db`, `quantity_mismatch`, `direction_mismatch`, `direction_unverified`) and, for unreadable checks, the cause (`broker_unavailable`, `db_unavailable`, `timeout`, `error:<Type>`). The check is bounded to 3 s and cannot raise into the entry.
- **`enforce` is available but not enabled.** It vetoes the entry (`reconciliation_unresolved`) when the book disagrees and (`reconciliation_unavailable`) when it cannot be checked; "could not check" is never "clean". It releases the entry's unsent claim and returns a blocked entry. Closes and emergency flattening are never evaluated in any mode. A value other than the three modes fails startup rather than silently meaning off.
- **A position that has just filled is explained, not vetoed.** The comparison reads only `open` rows, and a working order's row is `pending`, so a fresh fill would read as an untracked position for up to a fill-poll cycle. Untracked and size findings on a ticker with a pending row are recorded as explained; a pending row never explains a flipped position or a phantom row.
- **Drill it without trading.** `GET /api/admin/entry-gate` (operator session, read-only) runs the same evaluation now and reports what an entry would meet in each mode; `GET /api/admin/entry-gate/records` summarises what has been recorded. Neither sends, cancels, records or changes the mode.
- **Deployment:** backend restart only. No migration, no frontend change. The new setting defaults to `observe`; it adds one reconciliation read per entry (bounded to 3 s) and nothing else changes for entries. Deploy outside 09:30–16:00 ET. See the new runbook section for the criteria to meet before enabling `enforce`.
