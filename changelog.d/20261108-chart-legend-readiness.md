## Unreleased — chart legend no longer covers the price line; blocked setups say why on the row

- The chart legend (13 rows: MA5 … TP3) is collapsed by default on every screen size, behind a Legend button, instead of sitting permanently over the first part of the price line. The indicator readout above the chart is unchanged, and your choice is remembered in this browser.
- In Setup Readiness each row is now a real button with a chevron (keyboard accessible, announces expanded or collapsed), and a blocked or caution setup shows its first reason on the row itself, such as "Blocked: Daily timeframe disagrees…" with "(+N more)". Select the row for the full list and the unblock condition. Eligible rows show no reason line.

Frontend only: no backend restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
