## Unreleased — Edge Lab hypothesis ledger: write an idea down before testing it, and judge it honestly

- Adds a ledger for trading ideas (`/api/edge-ledger`, table `edge_hypotheses`, migration 0046). An idea is registered with its exact rule, universe, horizon, data plan and pass/fail criteria (a minimum sample of at least 30, a minimum effect AFTER costs, the cost, a baseline) and then locked: the registration columns cannot be edited, which the ORM enforces and tests pin. Whether the author had already seen the data must be stated (`retroactive`), with no default.
- Results are append-only and judged by the server against the criteria fixed beforehand. Costs come off first; a mean that does not cover them fails whatever its t-statistic. The t-statistic bar rises with every idea tried in the same family (Bonferroni: about 1.64 for one idea, 2.58 for ten, 3.29 for a hundred), so a lucky winner among many tries is discounted. `supported` and `not_supported` are final; a later look at the same question must be a new hypothesis, which raises the count. A verdict cannot be retired away.
- Numbers typed in by a person are never verified, matching the Research Lab's own provenance rule: they can end an idea but can never support one, and the caller cannot claim otherwise (the verified flag comes from the server-side source, never the request). No server-side evaluator exists yet, so today nothing can reach `supported`; an idea registered after its data was seen can reach at most `lead`, and only data dated after registration can confirm it. The first evaluator will ship with its own tests.
- A notebook with rules: it touches no orders, signals or risk, and does not replace the Research Lab, which remains the only path toward capital. All five routes need the operator key and are rate limited. No UI in this change.
- Needs `alembic upgrade head` (the standard deploy runs it) and a backend restart; no new environment variables.
