## Unreleased — 0DTE debit-spread proposal core and a windowed option-chain fetch (no orders, not wired in)

- Adds the first building block of a paper-only, human-in-the-loop 0DTE desk: a pure function (`app/services/zero_dte.py`) that turns one quoted option chain, a direction the operator chose and a risk budget into either a concrete debit-spread proposal or a list of reasons it refused. Only SPY and QQQ, only today's expiry, only 10:00-14:30 ET on a full trading day (early-close days are refused), and every check fails closed: a missing, crossed, wide, stale, future-dated or implausible quote is a refusal. Contracts are sized on the worst plausible fill (long ask less short bid), so the budget holds even if the order fills at the natural price, and a proposal carries a two-minute expiry. It imports nothing that can reach a broker, which a test pins, and nothing calls it yet: no route, no scheduler hook, no order path, no UI.
- `get_options_chain` gains optional `window`, `center` and `option_types` arguments so a caller that needs a few strikes near the money on one side no longer qualifies and quotes every strike on both sides (the 20-80 second step). IBKR honours them; Alpaca accepts and ignores them. Called without them, behaviour is unchanged.
- Needs a backend restart to load; no migration, no new environment variables, no behaviour change until a later change wires it in.
