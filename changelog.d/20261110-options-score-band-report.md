## Unreleased — Options score-band report (read-only script)

- New operator script `backend/scripts/options_score_band_report.py`: groups closed options trades by signal score (below 0.55, 0.55–0.62, 0.62–0.70, 0.70 and above) and shows, per band, how many closed, the win rate with a 95% interval, and average and total P&L, plus how many signals each band produced. It reports "insufficient data" instead of a comparison unless each of the two bands being compared has at least 30 closed trades, because a threshold cannot be judged from a handful of trades. It only reads the database: no writes, no orders, no change to any limit or threshold. Run it on the server with `python scripts/options_score_band_report.py --days 90` inside the backend container. Backend-only; no restart, no migration, no new environment variables.
