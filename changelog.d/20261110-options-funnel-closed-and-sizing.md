## Unreleased — Options funnel: closed-market attempts are not refusals, and "sized to zero" shows its numbers

- "Why options aren't trading" counted every execution attempt made while the market was closed (pre-market, after hours, weekends) as a block, so it could name "market_closed" the biggest reason even though execution pausing outside regular hours is by design. Those attempts now have their own neutral stage ("Attempted while the market was closed (not a refusal)"), "Refused at execution" counts only real refusals, and the verdict names the biggest real reason, adding how many closed-market attempts there were. If every attempt was made while closed, the verdict says so (MARKET CLOSED) instead of reporting a block. Dispatch is unchanged: the scanner still attempts around the clock and execution still refuses outside market hours.
- "Sized to 0 contracts" rejections now record the risk budget of one trade next to the spread's max loss, and the card shows one sentence comparing them (for example "One trade may risk about $400; a typical spread can lose $812 (2.0× the budget)"). Rejections recorded before this update carry no budget, so the card shows the max loss and says the budget is recorded from the next scan, not a guess. Nothing about sizing, limits or order submission changed. Needs a backend restart and a frontend rebuild; no migration, no new environment variables.
