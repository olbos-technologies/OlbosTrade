## Unreleased — Options scan: log where a slow scan is, and how long scan and dispatch take

- The scheduler stops the options scan after 240 seconds, and on a timeout the whole scan is cancelled, including the step that sends qualifying candidates on for execution. The timeout line could only say "last progress: unknown" for the options scan. It now records its progress (how many of the watchlist's symbols were scanned and how many failed, or which candidate was being dispatched, and the seconds elapsed), so a timeout reads for example "timed out after 240s — skipped (last progress: scanned 61/102 symbols (3 failed), 240s in)". Each cycle also logs one line when the scan finishes ("Options scan: N symbol(s) scanned in Xs (F failed), C candidate(s) to dispatch") and one when dispatch finishes. Nothing about what is scanned, ranked or dispatched changed; this is logging only. Needs a backend restart; no migration, no new environment variables.
