## Unreleased — Day P&L follows the US Eastern market day, and says it is realized

- "Day P&L" (Trade Desk header chip, Command Center, Executive Summary, Home) is the realized P&L of trades closed "today", but "today" was the server's UTC date. On a UTC server that date rolls over at 19:00 ET (20:00 in daylight time), so from 7 PM ET until midnight the figure read $0 even though trades had closed during that trading day, which made a day that had realized losses look flat. It now uses the US Eastern calendar day (a 24-hour day, 23 or 25 hours on the two daylight-saving changes), so a trading day is counted from 00:00 to 24:00 ET. The wording now says what the number is: realized P&L of trades closed today (ET), closed trades only, with open P&L not included, in the Command Center note, the Executive Summary line and a hover hint on the Trade Desk chip. The visible "Day P&L" labels are unchanged so the narrow-phone header keeps its layout. Only the dashboard display changed: the daily loss limit and the daily trade cap read the same server date as before and are unaffected in practice, since regular trading hours (09:30–16:00 ET) always fall inside one UTC date. Needs a backend restart and a frontend rebuild; no migration, no new environment variables.
